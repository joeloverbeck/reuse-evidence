# Recap

Use this before closing an interview or adjudication, accepting blanket approval, or handing
off to execution.

## Closing Recap

After the last fork, restate the ratified decisions, what the deliverable will contain, and
what remains out of scope. A fork answer is not itself the recap.

Scale the checkpoint to consequence:

- For small, reversible work, a separate one-line confirming beat may proceed immediately.
- For large reversible work, name the concrete changes before proceeding.
- For a new consequence that contradicts or reshapes a ratified decision, re-question that
  consequence.
- For hard-to-reverse or outward-facing work such as publishing, deploying, or sending, show
  the concrete action and leave a real veto window before acting.

When the user already saw the exact artifact, confirm that it is unchanged rather than
repeating it. A label-only choice does not count as an artifact preview.

Blanket approval ratifies only a recommendation that was already visible. Do not re-pose that
fork, but still give the scaled recap before execution.

If a self-contained verifier packet is being handed to another executor, the recap also gives
the administrator the acquisition run sheet required by
[Verifier Packets](verification-packets.md); keep blinded material out of the verifier-facing
prompt.
