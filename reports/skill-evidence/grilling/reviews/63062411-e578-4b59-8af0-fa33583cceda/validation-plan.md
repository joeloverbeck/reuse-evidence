## Risk tier and source-blind logistics

- Risk tier: high. A candidate touches the universal phase boundary controlling questions, product edits, handoffs, final delivery, and outward-facing safety.
- Fresh source-blind Codex executor per run, with user configuration, project rules, memory, and persistence disabled by runner controls.
- Opaque packages are `cairn` and `lumen`; their arm mapping is sealed in `administrator-custody.md`. Counted roots use neutral names `arm-sable` and `arm-ivory`.
- Executors receive one opaque complete package, one raw task, and named raw workspace artifacts only. They cannot read the evidence store, authoring rationale, the other package, previous output, evaluator material, network, or the real repository.
- Raw task and executor logistics remain separate. Logistics only locate `skill-package/`, `TASK.md`, and `workspace/`; they add no behavioral scope, output requirement, witness hint, or search directive.
- Candidate-arm unexpressed witnesses are replaced once with the same frozen task and a new executor; never discounted. An unexpressed first current reading stops only that mechanism.
- No packet field establishes a per-launch incidence estimate, so each fresh runnable reproduction uses the three-run floor.
- Process JSONL, final output, workspace artifacts, command shape, session identity, and hashes are retained. Any outside read, version-role leak, inherited context, missing output, or cross-run read invalidates the pair and requires a fresh opaque rerun.
