## Material proposal

- Human effect sought: keep long or constrained grilling runs from silently bypassing an applicable implementation gate, and keep a closing approval from deciding an independent fork by interpretation.

- Evidence bearing the claim: only the four trigger events in the preflight packet plus the exact-target predecessor report `reviews/270c958b-054f-4bf3-b6f8-8767ad5245a5.md`.
- Narrowest possible scope: candidate text only for mechanisms reproduced by the unchanged current arm; no repair of unrelated skill behavior.
- Out of scope: another owner, repository product code, general skill cleanup, incident capture, schema or CLI changes, tracker or network mutation, commits, and any second candidate after behavioral trials.
- Authority: the `ten_use_unresolved` preflight and claim, adopted repository principles, accepted ADR 0008, and the target's existing one-fork, subprocess-gate, implementation-workflow, and recap contracts.
- Falsification/parking: no candidate if the witnesses are unexpressed or current does not reproduce; reject if the candidate is not materially better, regresses a protected case, changes ownership or safety, or needs runtime growth unsupported by outcomes.
