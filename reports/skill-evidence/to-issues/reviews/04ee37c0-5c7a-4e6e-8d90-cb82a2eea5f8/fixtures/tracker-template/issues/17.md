State: OPEN

Comments: none

## Goal

Let a maintainer prepare an evidence handoff, validate it locally, and deliver it to a separate implementation workflow without losing privacy or exact decision content.

## User stories

1. Prepare a handoff from an accepted decision.
2. Preview the exact handoff before publication.
3. Refuse a handoff whose decision revision is stale.
4. Preserve private participant redaction.
5. Let the receiving workflow verify the accepted scope.
6. Resume after interruption without repeating approval.
