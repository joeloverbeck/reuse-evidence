# Use the Decision surface term for bounded human choices

## Consumer effect

Make it clear exactly what a maintainer is being asked to accept and what each choice authorizes, without implying that presenting choices constitutes acceptance.

## Authority

The project glossary in `CONTEXT.md` defines **Decision surface**. The term conforms to `docs/principles/FOUNDATIONS.md` §1 and `docs/principles/CAPABILITY-AND-WORKFLOW-BOUNDARIES.md` §10.

## Narrow scope

Use **Decision surface** for the exact bounded choices presented to the human together with each choice's authorized consequence. Keep the presentation distinct from the human act of acceptance.

## Out of scope

- Treating a proposal, preview, threshold, score, or presentation as acceptance.
- Expanding the choices beyond the live decision.
- Changing the recorded reuse-decision schema or acceptance mechanics.

## Acceptance

- User-facing decision requests identify the bounded choices and consequence of each.
- Documentation uses the glossary term consistently where this concept is meant.
- No path treats rendering or presenting a decision surface as human acceptance.
- Revisit the term if real usage cannot distinguish it reliably from a prepared proposal or reuse decision.
