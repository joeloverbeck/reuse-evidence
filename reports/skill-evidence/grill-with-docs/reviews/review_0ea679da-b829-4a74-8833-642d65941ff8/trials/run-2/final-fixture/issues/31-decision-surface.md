# Present an exact decision surface for human acceptance

## Consumer effect

A maintainer sees the exact bounded choices that remain open and the consequence each choice authorizes before any consequential case event is published.

The live bottleneck is human semantic authority: evidence and derived state can narrow a choice, but they cannot make it. The project glossary now names the boundary that capture and review must present consistently.

## Authority

- `CONTEXT.md` defines **Decision surface** as the exact bounded choices presented for human acceptance, with the consequence each choice authorizes, and states that presenting it is not acceptance.
- `docs/principles/FOUNDATIONS.md` §1 reserves semantic authority to the human.
- `docs/principles/CAPABILITY-AND-WORKFLOW-BOUNDARIES.md` §10 requires the exact proposal, evidence, revision, privacy consequence, allowed choices, and effect of each option before a consequential decision.
- `docs/principles/EVIDENCE-AND-DECISIONS.md` §§7 and 8 bound the review questions and permitted decision fields.

## Narrow scope

- In capture and reuse review, present only the choices still supported by the verified evidence and current case state.
- State the consequence each choice would authorize, including whether it records nothing, waits, proceeds to review, accepts a decision, or refuses.
- Keep presentation distinct from the later explicit act of human acceptance and expected-revision publication.

## Out of scope

- Treating presentation, preview, recommendation, silence, readiness, or occurrence count as acceptance.
- Expanding the permitted decision vocabulary beyond accepted authority.
- Designing implementation tasks or routing work to an engineering workflow.
- A new event, schema, command, or compatibility-promised rendering format.

## Acceptance

- Every consequential capture or review prompt identifies its bounded choices and the consequence of each.
- Tests or fixtures distinguish proposal presentation from explicit acceptance and prove that presentation alone writes no case event.
- A stale expected revision or unresolved privacy consequence prevents publication even when the same surface was previously presented.
- Park or falsify this issue if a real decision cannot be expressed within the accepted case vocabulary; amend the governing authority before widening the surface.
