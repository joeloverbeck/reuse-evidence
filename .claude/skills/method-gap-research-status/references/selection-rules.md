# Selection Rules

Use these rules only after the deterministic family inventory and
target-identifying lineage reconstruction are complete. They select a target for
a deeper census; they do not qualify a method gap or authorize research.

## Route non-method-gap evidence first

Do not select a target merely because it has:

- confusing, costly, or unreliable existing instructions;
- a documentation/implementation mismatch;
- an open or eligible Skill Evolution gate;
- a questionable pass/fail gate;
- analytical-to-human transfer uncertainty;
- a disputed goal, scope, or policy;
- elapsed time, many clean uses, a large package, or aesthetic unease; or
- no evidence store.

Route these respectively to ordinary skill iteration, conformance repair, Skill
Evolution, verdict calibration, paired transfer calibration, owner policy, no
action, or `insufficient evidence`. An out-of-distribution result is interval
evidence only until it exposes a plausible missing capability that external
prior art could address.

## Eligibility gates

A member can enter `screen next` only when all hold:

1. **Exact current target:** the canonical package exists and its current
   identity is known.
2. **Coherent baseline:** no overlapping method-gap line owns the target, no
   returned report awaits consumption, and current method-bearing changes can be
   frozen for a target-specific census.
3. **Material question:** concrete evidence identifies a consequence for an
   intended skill outcome, not merely inconvenience or style.
4. **Plausible missing capability:** the evidence resists the skill's ordinary
   internal routes or identifies an unexercised capability boundary. This is a
   hypothesis, not a conclusion.
5. **Useful target census:** inspecting the exact skill, lineage, operating
   envelope, and claim-instrument map could distinguish `commission now`,
   `postpone`, and `decline`.

If gates 1 or 2 cannot be determined, use `could not determine`. If gates 3 or 4
have some evidence but do not yet hold, use `watch`. If interval evidence is
absent or does not cover the current target, use `insufficient evidence`.

## Reject common false gaps

Before treating eligibility gate 4 as plausible, reject:

- **Renamed coverage:** equivalent machinery already exists under another term.
- **Optimization:** the capability exists; the proposal only makes it faster or
  more pleasant.
- **Feature wish:** the proposal expands the intended outcome instead of
  warranting it.
- **Framework shopping:** a named practice exists elsewhere but no local
  capability need is shown.
- **Evaluator-subject confusion:** a questionable verdict is treated as proof
  that its subject is defective.
- **Evidence-tier substitution:** human, analytical, or simulated evidence is
  allowed to overrule another tier without a transfer rule.

## Ordered positive signals

Compare eligible candidates by the first controlling signal below. The order is
lexical, not a point score:

1. A previously recorded deferred or reopening trigger has fired.
2. A stable out-of-distribution exercise exposed a plausible missing organ with
   material consequence.
3. Independent current evidence repeatedly resists existing categories or
   preserves a material `unknown` after ordinary ownership and conformance
   routes were considered.
4. A known gap or recurring expert judgment survives several target landings.
5. The skill now makes materially stronger claims, or operates under a
   materially changed population, scale, envelope, or consequence of error,
   than its evidence machinery was built to support.
6. A substantial adopted research line changed the baseline and enough later
   execution evidence now exists to audit the new delta.
7. Roughly ten meaningful versions have accumulated since the last audit.

Signal 7 is a heuristic and never wins over evidence that the versions were
nominal, the target is unstable, or a deferred trigger has not fired. Commit
count is not meaningful-version count. A first external audit may be a candidate
only when the target is stable and execution evidence or an explicit
missing-capability question supplies material pressure; `never audited` alone is
not a signal.

## Evidence comparison

Within the same controlling signal, prefer:

1. direct current evidence over retrospective or proxy evidence;
2. independent completed uses over repeated symptoms from one run;
3. material or severe consequences over ordinary friction;
4. evidence tied to the current target identity over old-hash evidence; and
5. an exact fired trigger or named capability boundary over general unease.

Do not break a genuine tie by target name, receipt count, report count, commit
count, or an invented weighted score. Place tied candidates in `watch`, explain
the missing discriminator, and select none.

## Status meanings

### `screen next`

Exactly one member has passed every eligibility gate and outranks every other
candidate through the ordered evidence rules. This status authorizes only the
census-only handoff printed by the parent skill.

### `watch`

A material signal may be developing, but recurrence, independence, present-hash
coverage, baseline stability, or capability-boundary evidence is not yet enough
to choose the member for target-specific census.

### `research in flight`

A target-specific brief awaits a report, a report awaits adjudication or
consumption, or an already authorized research/adjudication line owns the
decision. Do not duplicate it.

### `insufficient evidence`

No trustworthy interval exists for the current target identity. Missing receipts
are absence of knowledge, not a healthy result.

### `no current signal`

Trustworthy current interval and lineage evidence exist, but all observed
pressure routes to ordinary iteration or no positive cadence signal currently
applies. This is not a permanent `decline`.

### `could not determine`

Evidence integrity, target identity, lineage, in-flight ownership, or baseline
stability cannot be established safely.
